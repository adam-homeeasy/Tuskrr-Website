01 / PREMISE

Tuskrr’s buyer has upgraded everything except the bag, and the brand is built on that gap.

A leather work and everyday bag for the early-career climber in metro India. They changed the phone, the watch and the sneakers. They still carry the company backpack.

Every choice in this document is judged against that buyer.

02 / BUYER

The core buyer is an early-career climber in metro India, still carrying the company backpack.

Where

Lives in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Pune or Hyderabad. Works in tech, startups, consulting, design, media or finance. Smart-casual, hybrid, never a suit.

How they live

Commutes, flies between metros for work, gets out of the city at the weekend. Has upgraded the phone, the watch and the sneakers. The bag is still the company-issue black nylon backpack the whole floor carries.

How they buy

₹6,000 is affordable but still a decision. They wishlist it, then buy on payday, at a promotion or in a sale. For many, this is their first grown-up bag: the first good thing bought with their own salary.

Not for hikers, logo-seekers or suit-only formal buyers.

03 / GIFTING

Gifting is Tuskrr’s second front door, and it needs its own route through the site.

The gift giver

A partner, sibling or parent marking a first job, a promotion, a birthday or an anniversary. ₹5,000 to ₹7,000 is the sweet spot for a gift that feels significant, and a bag is used every working day, so the giver is remembered every day.

Corporate gifting

STRATA and CONTOUR for Diwali, work anniversaries and client gifts.

On the site: gift-ready box and dust bag, a printed note from the giver, initials embossed as a custom order (ready in 2 weeks), and one enquiry form for corporate orders. No Diwali page.

The site needs a gift route, not a gift-wrap checkbox.

04 / TRIGGERS

Two sentences start a sale, and both end in the same feeling.

The self-buyer

“I’ve levelled up. My bag hasn’t.”

The payoff is not owning the bag. It is the moment a colleague asks, “Where’s that from?”

The gift giver

“I see who you’re becoming.”

A gift that goes to every meeting, every flight, every day.

Both buyers pay for the same thing: pride in who you’re becoming.

05 / TAGLINE

Under the logo, on the hang tag, the site’s first screen and the end of every Reel.

In a gift note

06 / PERSONALITY AND VOICE

Tuskrr sounds confident, sharp, warm and a little wild, and it never calls itself premium.

Personality
IsIs not
Confidentloud

Voice rules
  • Second person. Talk to them, not about the bag.
  • Short, declarative lines. No exclamation marks.
  • Dry wit is welcome. Hype is not.
  • Never say premium, luxury, crafted with love or elevate.
  • The price is never a discount story. "Looks far more expensive than it is" is felt, never said.

Say less than you could, and let the leather carry the price.

09 / THEME 2

Theme two, The Entrance, stages every day as a walk into a room, lit by one door of light.

Arrive like
you mean it.
Look

Dark rooms, warm leather, the gold S as the only accent.

Signature

A single vertical band of warm light, the width of one stitched channel, runs through the brand like a door left ajar. Products stand in it. Headlines sit beside it. The gold S is the only other thing allowed to glow.

Voice

It is the direction built after the tagline, so it carries it at full weight.
