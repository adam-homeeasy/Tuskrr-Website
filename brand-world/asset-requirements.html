Delivery

File specs

Stills, master
TIFF or maximum-quality JPEG, sRGB, 3000px long edge minimum (4000px for macros).
Stills, web
AVIF and WebP at 640, 960, 1440 and 2048px wide, plus a JPEG fallback. Under 250 KB at 1440px.
Crops
Frame every product shot so 4:5, 1:1 and 9:16 can all be cut from it. Leave space above the bag for type.
Video, master
ProRes 422 or high-bitrate H.264, 4K or 1080p, 25 fps.
Video, web
H.264 MP4 and AV1 or VP9 WebM, 1080p, silent for loops. Loops under 2.5 MB, product loops under 1.5 MB.
Poster frames
One still per video at the web still spec, so pages work before and without video.
Colour
One grade across the set. Leather must match the real hide: shoot a colour card with every setup.
Naming
bag_view_ratio_version, for example ridge_front34_4x5_v1.jpg.
Releases
Signed model and location releases for everyone and everywhere shown.

Rights and rules

Before anything is shot

  • No stock photos or stock footage in any direction. Landscape loops are shot by us in India.
  • The moodboard photos in the product PDF are reference only; we do not hold their rights.
  • Reference sites are studied for structure and pacing only. None of their images, fonts, code or copy is used.
  • Label every image until the shoot lands: "Supplied render" or "Drawn stand-in".
  • Signed releases for every person and every location, including the street cast.
  • Leather colour must match the real hide. Shoot a colour card with every setup.

Not final

What is still open

  1. Nothing on this page is built. These are directions to choose from; the website is built only after you say go.
  2. Reference numbers come from reading each site’s shipped code (see brand-world/references/DNA.md). A live browser pass could not run in this session, so reveal travel and blur on reveals are not measured. Brunello Cucinelli blocked automated reading, so its direction uses our own motion numbers.
  3. Open from the handover: is the Linear Wilderness theme one of the round 1 worlds (Field Survey, Quiet Architecture, High Ground) or the idea on its own?
  4. Product images are the renders supplied by Tuskrr, shown as they are. They are not final photography.
  5. City scenes on these pages are drawn stand-ins, not photography. The real shoot should be in Indian cities with Indian models. The moodboard photos in the PDF are reference only and are not used, because we do not hold the rights to them.
  6. Laptop sizes and the returns policy are sample values, marked as samples, for layout only. Replace them with real figures before launch.
  7. Initials embossing is a custom order, ready in 2 weeks. The price is still to confirm.
  8. The logo is cut from the supplied JPEG files. Vector artwork is needed before anything goes to print or to the website.
  9. "Arrive like you mean it." needs a trademark search (IP India) before it is printed.
  10. Type choices are proposals. Every font shown is free for commercial use (SIL Open Font License).
  11. Voice samples illustrate tone. They are not approved copy.
