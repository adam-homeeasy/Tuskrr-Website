TUSKRR BRAND WORLD OPTIONS
==========================

1. Keep these four files together in one folder:
     START-HERE.html
     option-a-field-survey.html
     option-b-quiet-architecture.html
     option-c-high-ground.html

2. Double-click START-HERE.html. It opens in your web browser
   (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox). No internet connection is needed.

3. From START-HERE, open each option and scroll all the way down.
   Each one takes about three minutes. A laptop or desktop screen shows
   them best, but they also work on a phone.

4. Answer the five questions at the bottom of START-HERE.
   The first one matters most: which option, first instinct?

IF NOTHING MOVES
The pages fade in gently as you scroll. If they appear with no movement at
all, your computer has "reduce motion" switched on. That is fine, the pages
work either way. To see the motion anyway:
  - Mac: System Settings > Accessibility > Display > Reduce motion (turn off)
  - Windows: Settings > Accessibility > Visual effects > Animation effects (turn on)
  - iPhone: Settings > Accessibility > Motion > Reduce Motion (turn off)
Or add ?motion=force to the end of the address in the browser bar.

WHAT IS NOT FINAL
Product images are the supplied renders. Landscape art is drawn, not
photographed. The logo is cut from JPEG files and needs vector artwork.
Voice samples show tone only. Full list at the bottom of every page.
